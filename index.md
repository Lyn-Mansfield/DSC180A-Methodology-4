## Questions

1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?

The most interesting topic that we have covered so far has been how to compare completely disparate models by seeing how much their representational geometries align with each other. This is interesting because the way each model represents the same input can be entirely arbitrary. What matters is if the differences between those representations given different inputs mirror each other, which is a brilliant solution to the problem. 

2. Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.

One possible avenue of investigation would be to see how different models of mouse brain activity differ in their internal representations of what is going on in the mouse's brain. This would require interpreting the ways that each method models behavior and comparing the different ways they go about representing how to decode/encode brain activity. 

3. What is a potential change you’d make to the approach taken in your current Quarter 1 Project?
